- **Shipped vs corrected, in one line each:**
  - Cube 0.35 kg (22,400 kg/m³, #22) becomes **0.01125 kg** (painted hardwood at 720 kg/m³; band 0.0094–0.0166 kg, hardwood low to solid ABS). Its push resistance on the table drops from 1.72 N to about 0.03 N (μs 0.308 at map v2), about 50 times lower.
  - Cup 0.15 kg becomes 0.030 kg (moulded PP, the real cups). 0.15 kg is what the mesh weighs in porcelain (0.159–0.173 kg).
  - Engine inertia today belongs to a different body than the mass (#113). Corrected: cube I = 1.17e-6 kg m² (shipped 1.63e-6), cup I ≈ 2.7e-5 kg m² (shipped 7.8e-5).
- **Two class decisions:**
  1. **Cubes (decided, #167 decision 1):** the class table says aluminium (low; VLM caption "polished metal / reflective aluminum"). The cubes now follow the real frames, like the cup: painted wood (class `hardwood`; toy blocks are usually beech) primary, `abs_toy_plastic` alternative. Why wood over ABS: in the head frame (about 10 px per cube face) the cubes are matte with no specular highlight while the PP cups and the aluminium arm show highlights, and the yellow cube has an uneven, grain-like tone. This is weak evidence and favours wood only slightly. Aluminium stays only as a named sensitivity. `cube_material` (ABS 0.0166 kg; aluminium 0.042 kg) runs as G4 cells outside `docs/params`.
  2. **Cup:** caption says glazed ceramic; the class table says polyolefin (real frames). The slice follows the class table. The real cup is also a different shape (rounded square, taller than wide) from the 72 mm round tumbler mesh. That is an R gap, not a parameter.

1. Cups stand mouth-down at x = −0.20, 0, +0.20 m, y = −0.230. Cubes stand at y = −0.13, 100 mm in front of the cups. The six layout files are the six colour permutations (red: 53/60/52 at x = −0.2/0/+0.2).
2. Cube `mass: 0.35` in all 495 cube slots, as in metadata (#22). Cup `mass: 0.15` in all 495 cup slots. Both are delivered, under the 0.5 kg cap.
3. The `friction: 0.25` key is dead (#1). Code default 0.6/1.5 (`rigid.py:84-90`); effective 0.5/0.5 on cpu (#58). Under the order fix alone, cube–table would become 1.0 and the cube would hop (findings D3). At the corrected realised 0.31 (map v2) a sliding cube does not tip: the tip limit is μ > half-width/COM height = 1.

5. Table 1.4 × 1.1 m (`default.yml:7-11`) and Ground 1.0/1.0 multiply, as in every non-bottle task.
