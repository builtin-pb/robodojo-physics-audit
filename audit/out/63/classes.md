## Notes and cautions

- `Rigid/cup/00001` (cover_blocks only) is `polyolefin_plastic` from the real-robot frames; its synthetic caption says glazed ceramic (flag `real_vs_sim_material_conflict`).
- `polyolefin_plastic` is one class; `polymer_subtype` (pe_probable 4, pp 1, pp_probable 15, unspecified 5) records PP/PE where a caption, the category or the real frames state it.
- `abs_toy_plastic` and `abs_device_housing` are hard-thermoplastic families (ABS/PS/PVC/PC), not one polymer; only 5 of their models have a caption naming a family polymer.
- Contact material is the grasped surface: handles of tools (pliers, scissors, whisk, peeler) and pads of headphones carry the alternatives listed per model.
- Garment/Top_Long is cloth particles; Fluid/wuliangye is the liquid, Rigid/wuliangye the bottle; Dynamic/conveyor is a 4 m belt driven kinematically.
- Geometry fixtures are classed by their real analogue; their declared mass is often 0 and unused (static colliders).
