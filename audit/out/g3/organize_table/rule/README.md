## 3. Shared Contact Helper & `v2_contact` Rule Specification

### 3.1 Shared Helper (`src/eval_client/contact_v2.py`)
- **What it reads:** On each physics substep inside `EvalEnv.step` (`src/eval_client/eval_env.py:854-858`), `SubstepContactRecorderV2` reads `get_physx_simulation_interface().get_contact_report()` for prims carrying `PhysxContactReportAPI(threshold=0.0)` (`SceneManager.create_object_from_config` at `env/scene_manager/scene_manager.py:322-327`), maps actor USD paths to logical labels (`alarm`, `mouse`, `garage`, `keyboard`, `drawer`, `mousemat`, `cube_cushion`, `table`, `robot{r}/link7`, `robot{r}/link8`, or raw stage prim path), converts contact positions into the env-local world frame (`position - env_origin`), and records tracked rigid poses plus target robot jaw gaps (`0.00081 + q7 + q8`).
- **Guards (`#209` generalized to `contact_v2.py`):**
  1. `assert_v2_contact_decimation(env)` raises `ValueError("... requires decimation == 1 ...")` at task `__init__`, `_post_setup_scene`, `reset`, `EvalEnv.step`, and `SubstepContactRecorderV2.record_substep_contacts` whenever `_v2_contact_active` is `True` and any of `sim_config.decimation`, `sim_cfg.decimation`, or `sim.cfg.decimation` differs from `1` (leaving `v1` and `v2/v1` unconstrained).
  2. `sync_physx_contact_processing_v2(env, active=...)` never imports or mutates `carb.settings` when `active=False` from startup; when `active=True`, it saves `/physics/disableContactProcessing` and sets `False`; when an env resets out of `v2_contact`, it restores the saved flag before `super().reset()` (so settle substeps run with the restored setting).

### 3.2 `v2_contact` Subtask Terms (`src/eval_client/evaluator_v2.py:1946-2308`, `env/reward_manager/func_parser.py:1322-1431`)
Every subtask keeps all shipped `v1` terms (with `#184` `StillnessSnapshotV2` active under `v2`) and appends two contact predicates evaluated over the current action's `ActionContactWindowV2`:
1. **`is_object_not_held_v2(label)`:** Fail-closed (`False` if `0` substeps, incomplete frame, or non-finite jaw reading); returns `True` iff on every substep of the action window, no target robot (`r in {0, 1}`) simultaneously has contact impulse `>= 1e-5` N*s on both `robot{r}/link7` and `robot{r}/link8` with `label`.
2. **Target-surface contact support on an unheld substep (`substep_filter = _unheld`, `min_impulse_ns = 1e-5` N*s):**
   - **Alarm (`is_alarm_supported_by_drawer_top_v2`):** `alarm`'s bottom bbox center in `drawer` local frame lies inside `[-0.06130, +0.19226] x [-0.19195, +0.16857]` m (`drawer/00000` upward-facing top rim `[-0.05630, +0.18726] x [-0.18695, +0.16357]` m expanded by `margin_m = 0.005` m, excluding the mid-height front pull handle `x in [-0.07988, -0.06425]` m) **AND** `alarm <-> drawer` has a contact point inside that local XY box with `lz >= 0.190` m on an unheld substep.
   - **Mouse (`is_object_supported_by_target_top_v2("mouse", "mousemat")`):** `mouse <-> mousemat` has a contact point inside `mousemat`'s local XY bbox `+ 0.005` m with `lz >= -0.005` m (`Geometry/mousemat/00000`'s flat pad surface sits at `lz = -0.00256` m inside its `[-0.003634, +0.003634]` m wrist-rest bbox) on an unheld substep.
   - **Figurine (`is_object_supported_by_target_top_v2("garage", "cube_cushion")`):** `garage <-> cube_cushion` has a contact point inside `cube_cushion`'s local XY bbox `+ 0.005` m with `lz >= -0.002` m on an unheld substep.
   - **Keyboard (`is_keyboard_supported_by_table_v2("keyboard", "frame")`):** `keyboard <-> table` has a contact point within `|dx| <= 0.30` m, `|dy| <= 0.18` m, `|dz| <= 0.012` m of `frame`'s pose on an unheld substep.
   - **Meaning of `"supported"` in `v2_contact.1` (`audit/gates.json`):** In this version, `"supported"` means at least one qualifying contact point with the target in the action window (on an unheld substep, with `min_impulse_ns = 1e-5` N*s applied per point), not measured load-bearing support share.

### 3.3 Provenance, Stamping & Review Non-Blocking Fixes
- **Rule & version stamps (`evaluator_v2.py`):** `ORGANIZE_TABLE_RULES = ("v1", "v2_contact")` (`ORGANIZE_TABLE_RULE_DEFAULT = "v1"`). `_result.json` and `_resume_<run_id>.json` stamp `organize_table_rule`, `organize_table_rule_version` (`"v1.0"` or `"v2_contact.1"`), and `organize_table_params` (and likewise `xylophone_strike_rule`, `xylophone_strike_rule_version` `"v1.0"` or `"v2_contact.2"`, and `xylophone_strike_params` for `play_Xylophone`). `organize_table_stamp_v2` and `xylophone_strike_stamp_v2` compare `(rule, rule_version, params)` on resume and multi-seed aggregation (`aggregate_v2_benchmark`). Non-`v1` records missing `rule_version` or `params` fail closed (`valid=False`, task invalid) and receive distinct `unstamped_nonce` stamps so uniformly unstamped `v2_contact` records never match each other, while legacy unstamped `v1` files remain compatible. Passing `ROBODOJO_ORGANIZE_TABLE_RULE=v2_xy` raises `ValueError`; loading an archived `v2_xy` record sets `valid=False` (`retired_or_unknown_organize_table_rule:v2_xy`), and feeding the retired `g3org_pre_w3_v2xy` (`25.0`) + `g3org_f2_w3_v2xy` (`0.0`) pair as two seeds returns `valid=False, score_mean=None, invalid_reason="mixed organize_table rules"`.
- **Non-blocking fixes:** (1) `self.organize_table_rule` is fixed once in `OrganizeTableCommon.__init__` and reused in `eval_env.py` (`_result.json` / `_resume_<run_id>.json`) without re-reading `os.environ`; (2) `witness_organize_table.py` docstring accurately describes clamping vs attempted modes; (3) `test_evaluator_v2.py` tests unconfounded `seed 0` (`expected_seed=0`) vs `seed 1` (`expected_seed=1`) records where each seed record is individually `valid=True`.
