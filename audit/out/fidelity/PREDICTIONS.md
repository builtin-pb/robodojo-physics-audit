# Harness fidelity: predictions (committed before each batch)

Question: is each blog finding RoboDojo's behaviour, or could the harness have produced it? Three paths run the same policy on the same layout, all on della-vis2 GPU 0 (A40, Isaac Sim 5.1, Isaac Lab `afca7b0`), shipped physics, device `cpu`, seed 0:

- `fh_`: the harness, `audit/harness/run_harness.py` (its own driver loop around `EvalEnv.reset` / `run_eval`, manifest readback after reset, physics-step counter, optional contact reports).
- `fm_`: RoboDojo's entry point `src/eval_client/main.py` of this fork, launched by `audit/harness/run_main_exploit.py`.
- `fu_`: upstream RoboDojo `f57e163`'s `main.py` and all its code, with only this branch's `audit/` folder added, through the same launcher (`--upstream` drops the `--evaluator` flag that upstream lacks).

What the comparison can and cannot separate. `fm_` and `fh_` share the physics-only Kit experience, the stubs (null cameras that keep the one camera-reset step and the per-env reseeding, the MDL helper, cuRobo, the WebSocket client) and the `PARTIAL_RENDERING` emulation. So `fh_` against `fm_` tests the harness's own driver and instrumentation; `fm_` against `fu_` tests the fork's edits to RoboDojo's code (22 files, including `eval_env.py`, `rigid.py`, `reward_manager.py`, `func_parser.py` and `cover_blocks.py`) at the shipped preset and v1 evaluator; neither tests the stubs or the Kit experience. A full rendering evaluation is not possible on della-vis2 (Isaac Sim 5.1's RTX renderer segfaults on driver 615.71; the full RTX kit dies with "Out of resource descriptors", #93, #120). For those, the evidence is by argument and prior matches (#40 D4: Isaac Sim 6.2 with its renderer matches the 5.1 harness exactly on bottles layout 0).

Comparison (`audit/tools/fidelity_compare.py`): per action, every rigid object's position and quaternion at the logged resolution (1e-5 m and 1e-5, the harness's `--log_poses` rounding; `fm_`/`fu_` also log unrounded values), joint positions, stages left and success flag; settled and final poses; score, success and action count; the `_result.json` that `EvalEnv.run_eval` writes on each path.

## Revisions after the design critique (before batch 2; batch 1 was already running)

An independent critic read the design (not the expected verdict). Changes:

- Scope of a pass. `fh_` = `fm_` shows that the harness's driver and instrumentation did not produce a result; `fm_` = `fu_` shows that the fork's edits did not. What all paths share can still hide an artefact: the physics-only Kit, the stubs, the launcher's carb settings and render emulation, the `need_planner` patch, the policy itself, and (in batch 1) the pose logging. Results are stated that way, not as "not a harness artefact".
- `queue.sh` now marks a `main.py` run done only when it exits 0, clears `ROBODOJO_*` variables before each run (none were set on della-vis2 for batch 1: checked), sets `ROBODOJO_FATAL_RESTART_COUNT=3` so that a fatal PhysX failure exits with rc 99 instead of `main.py`'s `os.execv` self-restart (which would run without the stubs), and samples GPU memory every second during each run. The compare tools flag missing layouts or rows. The upstream leg is a commit (`dfe5bd0`, then rebuilt with this `audit/`): `git diff f57e163 <leg> -- . ':!audit'` is empty, and both checkouts' `Assets` link to the same folder.
- `run_main_exploit.py --log_poses` now counts physics steps as the harness does. Prediction: steps from the start of the process (or the end of the previous episode) to the first action equal the harness's `steps_through_reset` for the same task, and every action is 10 steps.
- Pose logging is no longer on in every arm: `fm_presslit_nolog` runs `fm_presslit` without it. Prediction: final poses (now recorded on every episode, read after the policy returns) identical to `fm_presslit`.
- Contact reports applied after the body is built. The harness adds `PhysxContactReportAPI` to each rigid object after RoboDojo has built it, on exactly the bodies whose delivered friction, mass and inertia are D1, D8, D9 and V1; the committed sweep readbacks (`vb_sweep_*`) were made with it and with the scene-query BVH. `fm_rb_*` have neither. Prediction: identical engine values (materials, masses, inertias at the harness's 3 significant digits). A difference would make those findings harness artefacts.
- G1 from the `main.py` logs, no extra runs: whenever a finger is blocked by an object it grips (`fm_vase`'s cup, `fm_xylo`'s mallet, `fm_peek`'s cups), the logged joint7/joint8 target sits about 10.8 mm inside the measured position at the end of an action, so the drive force read from the logged targets is at most about 24.8 N (2300 N/m × 10.8 mm).
- Committed harness runs from older commits (`x2_vase_L0_v1`, `x2_xylo_L0_v1c`, `vb_shv_buttons_eval`, `rv101_52_*`) are compared as they are; RoboDojo's code is unchanged since them, but `exploits.py` and `run_harness.py` are not. If one differs from its `main.py` run, a fresh `fh_` twin runs before any interpretation, and `fm_` is rerun first (the zero noise floor is shown for the harness path only).
- `witness_cb` passes `inverted_cup_v2` to the predicates, which upstream ignores, so predicate values in the policy logs may differ between `fm_peek` and `fu_peek` by construction; those runs are compared on physics, evaluator flags and results only.
- Not reachable through `main.py` at all, so argued from the deviations: findings made with harness-only policies or settings (force_curve and ideal_witness for G1, G2, G4; the slide refinement of S1; the binding-order counterfactual of D3; the device switch of D2) and A13's failing side (#114 needs a GPU with about 2 GB free; GPU 0 now has about 27 GB). `fm_hold_screws` and `fh_fasten_drop` are two positive controls, not a fidelity pair.
- Step windows (added after batch 1 showed 986 steps from process start to the first action in `fm_presslit` against the harness's 984 through `env.reset()`, while poses were identical; bottles gave 844 on both): `--log_poses` now also counts the steps inside `create_eval_env` and inside each `env.reset()`. Prediction: `env.reset()` alone takes 984 steps on press_by_number layout 0, as in the harness, and the 2 extra steps happen in `create_eval_env`, which the harness also runs but does not count; if instead they fall inside the reset, the paths differ by two physics steps before the episode, which the identical poses would then show to be harmless for this layout only.
- Budget trims of batch 2 (`repro/q2.txt` comments): `fm_presspro` without `--log_poses`; prediction: success, 100, 307 actions, final poses equal to the last row of the committed harness run `x2_press_pro_L0_v1b` (logged every action). `fm_xylo_c` and `fm_hold_screws` are not run unless needed. The queue stops at 1.45 GPU-hours in total.

## Batch 3 (`repro/q3.txt`), after batch 2's step windows

`fm_buttons` counted 0 steps in `create_eval_env` and 986 inside `env.reset()`, against 984 (`fh_presslit_L0`) and 972 (`vb_shv_buttons_eval`) in the harness for the same layout, with identical counters and robot joints. RoboDojo's `ArticulationObject` calls `omni.kit.app.get_app().update()` itself at construction, `apply_saved_pose` and `relocate_offscreen` (upstream `articulation.py:254,285,310`), outside `render()`, while the timeline plays and with fixed time stepping off (Isaac Lab's AppLauncher setting, which evaluation uses). Prediction: in two identical `main.py` runs, those calls take physics steps (more than 0 in total), the totals differ between the runs, and the difference equals the difference in steps through reset; every other part of the reset takes the same number of steps. If the calls take no steps, the varying count has another source, to be found before anything is claimed.

## Outcomes (written after the runs; numbers in `summary.json`)

- Batch 1: all three pairs identical at the logged resolution, with equal `_result.json` files and the predicted scores (25 and 40; 0 with blue 1 of 2; 100 and 100); fork and upstream bit-identical unrounded. Predicted at 70 to 85%; held.
- Batch 2: #123 and #124 identical to the committed harness runs per action, with the predicted scores and numbers (balls 7.2 to 46.6 mm outside the wall; head 15.4 to 18.7 mm above the bars), so `fm_xylo_c` was not needed; #125's protocol 100 at 307 actions without pose logging, every press as in the harness; #111's counters identical at every change, failed at 236, monitor silent; all readbacks equal to the committed harness values, fork equal to upstream; slides 24.549 and 24.722 mm; A7's two witness readings identical; #114's positive control healthy (no corruption, nuts land, grippers 0.044). G1: the prediction (a lead of about 10.8 mm, at most about 24.8 N) was exceeded: steady-grip medians 10.84 to 10.91 mm but maxima up to 11.49 mm (26.4 N), and 25.4 mm (58 N of spring force) while a gripped cup moved the finger in the vase pour. The summary also first dropped the nuts' measured settle time; it is 0.08 s (sampled every 0.02 s).
- Step windows: the reset itself took 986 steps on `main.py` (0 in `create_eval_env`), so the 2-step gap was not in construction, contrary to the batch 2 prediction.
- Batch 3: RoboDojo's articulation update calls in `apply_saved_pose` and `relocate_offscreen` take a constant 10 steps each (90 per reset; construction 0) in both runs while the resets took 981 and 986 steps; the prediction that they carry the variation was wrong. Stopped there (two probes, no verdict moved).
- Batch 4: the coin read 0.5 kg with the harness's inertia, as predicted.
- Budget: about 0.6 GPU-hours predicted, 1.334 used (episodes about 1.5 times slower than in round 2 on the shared GPU).
