November 13, 2026. Goal: a real policy through RoboDojo's own evaluation loop on Isaac Sim 6.2, and a check that shipped physics there reproduces the policy's published 98% on put_bottles_into_dustbin, before any corrected-physics run.

## Where it stands

- **Gate: 20 of 20 at shipped physics on Isaac Sim 6.2, against the published 98%.** The run (`runs/gate20_shipped_v1_ngx`) used seed 0, layouts 0 to 19, evaluator v1, on della-vis2 GPU 1.
  - main.py's own `_result.json`: success rate 1.0, score 100, with all 20 layouts present and none dropped.
  - The 95% Clopper–Pearson interval is 0.832 to 1.0, which contains 0.98 (the leaderboard's 147 of 150). The pre-registered pass was 18 or more. If the true rate were 0.98, 20 of 20 would have probability 0.67.
  - Every episode ended before the 700-action limit (271 to 504 actions), so each ended through the task check.
  - Timing on a shared A40: 248 s per episode on average (112 to 375 s). Policy calls had a median of 0.87 s, observations took 0.18 s and actions 0.24 s.
  - Peak memory: policy 29,070 MiB, simulator 5,594 MiB. The GPU never fell below 4,436 MiB free. No PhysX corruption or allocation-failure lines.
  - NGX was on in all 20 episodes: `/ngx/enabled` true and DLAA (`aa/op` 4) read back at every episode's first observation. The denoiser flag reads true even without NGX (P3), so the grain, not that flag, shows the denoiser ran.
  - The ISO 80 exposure held only in episode 0. Every later reset restored Kit's default of 100 (read back at each first observation). So 19 of 20 episodes ran at the declared settings with default exposure. Their head frames are 10 to 15% brighter than the demonstrations' median (median 12%), and the policy succeeded in all of them (19 of 19; 95% lower bound 0.82). `main62.py` now reapplies overrides after every reset, and the call order puts it before each episode's first observation, but no run has tested that yet.
  - Corrected physics was not run.
- **Render noise fixed (a delivery fault in our container setup, not RoboDojo's; #158, `docs/findings.md` I9).** The grain came from apptainer's `nvliblist.conf` on Della (apptainer 1.5.5; della-vis2 and the login node have the same list, so it is probably apptainer's stock list). It binds `libnvoptix` into the container but not the driver's `libnvidia-ngx.so.1`, so NGX could not start (`render_check/ngx_cause.txt`). Without NGX, 6.2's Real-Time path tracing ran without the DLSS-RR denoiser that RoboDojo's `quality` preset turns on (`rtx-transient.dldenoiser.enabled`), and the declared DLAA, which also needs NGX, could not run. Kit ships the DLSS feature libraries itself. `run_g5.sh` now binds the host's `/lib64/libnvidia-ngx.so.1` (resolved to `/usr/lib64/libnvidia-ngx.so.615.71.10`) into `/.singularity.d/libs/`, and stops if the library is missing. The NGX errors are gone. The bind alone is the cause. Probe P3 is P1 with `NGX=0` on the same GPU and code path. It ran at `100c4c2`, whose `main62.py` is the gate's; only `run_g5.sh`'s missing-library stop differs. It fails to create the NGX context and reads TAA (`/rtx/post/aa/op` 1) and `/ngx/enabled` false at its captures. Its head grain is 23.4, against P1's 1.9 (`runs/probe_ngx_p3_off/`; predictions in `PREDICTIONS.md` §6). At each episode's first observation, `/rtx/post/aa/op` reads 4 (DLAA, as declared) and `/ngx/enabled` true. `/rtx/post/dlss/execMode` 2 and the DL denoiser flag also read as declared, but they read the same without NGX (P3), so the grain is what shows the denoiser ran. This overturns the earlier claim (#40, the #106 clips, the first draft of this README) that NGX "cannot start in the container" (`docs/research/lessons.md`).
- **Render look against the training images** (table below; `render_check/`). The reference is 45 frames of five bottle demonstrations, each camera at 0, 25 and 50% of the episode, decoded as XPolicyLab decodes them. Grain is the mean absolute difference of each pixel from its 3×3 median. 6.2 frames are measured after JPEG at the demonstrations' own quality (95, 4:2:0), so both sides carry the same compression. Head-camera grain fell from 23.6 to 2.02 over the gate's 20 first frames (1.80 to 2.11), against the demonstrations' 1.38 to 1.61. Denoised, the head camera was 14% brighter than the demonstrations (90.6 against a median of 79.7; red +14%, green and blue +13%). Neither Isaac Lab nor RoboDojo sets exposure, so the difference comes from the two renderers' defaults or lighting, not from a RoboDojo value. No 5.1 render of the same layout exists to separate scene from renderer (5.1 cannot render on Della's driver, #40). A pre-registered sweep of `/rtx/post/tonemap/filmIso` (`PREDICTIONS.md` §4) picked ISO 80: head 80.1, channel ratios to the demonstrations 1.02, 0.99, 0.99. The red-to-green balance is 3.1% above the demonstrations', against 1.1% at ISO 100, so exposure alone does not match the colour exactly. The gate was to run with it as a declared deviation, but it held only in episode 0 (head 80.1). Each later reset restored the default ISO 100, and episodes 1 to 19 read 87.7 to 91.5. The wrist cameras cannot be matched this way: their brightness depends on the object in view (demonstrations 46 to 180). In motion, a wrist close-up of a moving bottle shows some smearing at edges, likely the temporal denoiser, not tested (`render_check/gate_E0_o150_cam_left_wrist.png`). Grain also counts real edges and grows with brightness. Divided by mean brightness, it is 2.1 to 2.4% on the fixed probe frames against 1.74 to 1.98% on the demonstrations' head frames, so 6.2 stays somewhat above the demonstration range (the independent check's computation).

## Runs on della-vis2

`audit/harness/g5/` and `docs/REPRODUCE.md` section 8 hold the recipe. `run_g5.sh` runs the adapter's own smoke test (optional), starts the adapter's own `setup_eval_policy_server.sh`, and runs RoboDojo's `main.py` unmodified through `main62.py` inside the 6.2 container with the flags `scripts/eval_policy.sh` passes, plus `--num_envs 1 --evaluator v1 --physics_preset shipped`. Before each launch it compares the GPU's free memory with what the next process needs plus a reserve left to co-tenants (these runs: policy 30,500 MiB, simulator 6,000 MiB, reserve 2,000 MiB, set per run and recorded in `run_info.txt` from `4c512bd` on; the launcher's defaults are 34,000, 6,000 and 3,000), and stops rather than crowd the GPU. Seed 0; predictions and decision rules were committed before any run (`PREDICTIONS.md`, section 0 for the A40). Wall time counts as GPU time; the A40 was shared throughout (other users' jobs at 70 to 100% utilization, Bo-side harness queues at about 2 GB), so timings are not those of a dedicated GPU.
